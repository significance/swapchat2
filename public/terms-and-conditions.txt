HOW IT WORKS

Messages sent through Swapchat are end-to-end encrypted using a hybrid cryptographic scheme combining classical elliptic curve key exchange (ECDH) with post-quantum key encapsulation (ML-KEM-768). Only you and your chat partner can read your messages.

Encrypted messages are stored as chunks on the Swarm network. Because Swarm is decentralized, these chunks may be stored by any node operator in any jurisdiction. Data persistence on Swarm is not guaranteed. Chunks may expire or become unavailable at any time.
